base.obj: MakeHuman base mesh (hm08), CC0 (released Sept 2020), from the MakeHuman asset repository.
neutral.obj: genderless, faceless mannequin made from base.obj by neutralize.py. Same coordinate frame/units and same 19158 vertices in the same order as base.obj (body = 0..13379; helper vertices kept but unreferenced); only group 'body' faces kept, face list identical to base.obj's body group.
  Head (11 Nov revision): the base head is kept: skull, ears, jaw/chin, cheekbones, brow ridge and neck-jaw line are unchanged (ears, skull sides, back and top of skull, neck: 0 displacement). Only a front patch over eyes, nose and lips (2450 vertices, incl. the mouth/eye/nostril cavities behind it) is re-laid fold-free in the front (x,y) plane (uniform Tutte map, no flipped triangles) and lifted onto the base face's own depth map, morphologically closed (fills eye slits, nostrils, mouth line), opened along y (removes the lips) and blurred, with a harmonic join to the untouched skin plus a light Taubin seam pass. Result: a sculpted mannequin face form (brow, eye hollows, cheeks, muzzle, a soft nose ridge) with no eyes, nostrils or lips.
  Body (unchanged from the previous version): breasts removed by a fold-free re-layout plus thin-plate-spline ribcage; groin biharmonic-smoothed; hips 5% narrower, seat pulled in. Hands, feet, arms, legs untouched (0 displacement).
